## Figures

This section provides a brief overview of each figure in the repository.

### Figure 1
**Description:** Glacial lakes in the study area for water-based indices comparison.

### Figure 2
**Description:** Heatmaps illustrating comparing the changes in overall accuracy (%) and kappa hat statistic when changing the number of trees and the number of training points per class showing for the training and testing image. 

### Figure 3
**Description:** Heatmaps illustrating comparing the changes in overall accuracy (%) and kappa hat statistic when changing the number of trees and the number of training points per class showing for the OBIA classification.

### Figure 4
**Description:** The study area image and the comparison of the binary OBIA classification map before and after applying additional input features.  

### Figure 5
**Description:** The original training image, multi-class OBIA classification trained on the original training image and The multi-class OBIA classification trained on five images.

### Figure 6
**Description:**  The comparison of the number of misclassified pixels before and after applying input features.

### Figure 7
**Description:**  Kernel density estimate (KDE) plot to visualise the distribution of band values for 8 and 12-bit resolution sensors, Landsat 9 and all sensors for water. 

### Figure 8
**Description:** Kernel density estimate (KDE) plot to visualise the distribution of bands and indices values for the different land classes for Landsat 9.

### Figure 9
**Description:** The comparison of five different images with the binary OBIA classification for one image trained against 5 images trained.

### Figure 10
**Description:** Glacial lake area (km2) for the Tasman glacial lake using the OBIA, pixel-based indices and manual delineation.
